Before you order.

A few things that protect John's money and his deadline.

Get the date in writing

Ask for the confirmed delivery window at checkout, not the "estimated ship" date. If it isn't on or before Nov 2, ask what's in local stock instead.

Firm, but not too firm

Side sleepers put their weight on the shoulder and hip. A medium-firm bed (about 6–7 out of 10) with a pillowtop keeps the spine straight without numb arms. Truly firm beds tend to press on the shoulder.

Check the trial rules

Most trials ask you to sleep on the bed 30 nights before returning, and store returns can carry a pickup or restocking fee. Read the policy before signing.

Plan for the old mattress

Saatva removes the old bed for free. Stores usually charge for removal and setup, so add it to the order if he needs it.

Measure the stairs

A 14"+ king is heavy and stiff. Boxed beds like the Helix and WinkBed are easier to carry upstairs than full-size store mattresses.

Keep the law tag

Leave the label on and keep the receipt. Warranties need both.
